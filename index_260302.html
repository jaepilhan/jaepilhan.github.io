I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Economics at Chungnam National University. My research focuses on innovation economics, spatial econometrics, regional development, and productivity analysis. Formerly, I was an Assistant Professor at Gachon University and a Research Fellow at the Korea Development Institute (KDI).

Research

My research spans spatial econometrics methodology, firm-level innovation and digital transformation, regional economic development, and energy policy. I develop econometric tools for analyzing spatially interdependent economic phenomena and apply them to policy-relevant questions.
